# Multibonk privacy and cookie summary

Effective 1 November 2026. This summary accompanies package 0.2.8; it covers
the website, voluntary contact form, and published DLL v0.2.5 / protocol 111.
Read the [full privacy notice](https://multibonk.ludix.gg/privacy/) and
[cookie notice](https://multibonk.ludix.gg/cookies/) for more detail.

## Operator and contact

The project is maintained by **AvinadavCoh for TeamAvi, Israel**. Use the
[private request form](https://multibonk.ludix.gg/#privacy-request) and choose **Privacy request**
to ask about access, correction, deletion, objections or other applicable rights.
A reply address is needed for a response. Provide enough information to locate
your message, such as its title/date; do not send identity documents unless a
necessary, proportionate verification step is explained. Rights and exceptions
depend on applicable law. The maintainer's inbox address is not public.

## Website visits and cookies

The application does not set first-party cookies, use browser storage, or load
advertising, analytics or session-replay trackers. Fonts and images are
self-hosted. Cloudflare delivers and protects the site and processes IP
addresses, URLs, browser/network information, timestamps and operational/security
logs. The report endpoint uses IP addresses for short-lived rate limiting.
Cloudflare may use necessary security cookies or similar mechanisms; this is not
a promise that infrastructure never uses cookies or retains request metadata.
External sites such as GitHub, Thunderstore and Steam have their own policies.

## Voluntary reports and reply addresses

Browsing and downloading do not require sending a message. A report includes
your title/description, mod version and host/client role; a reply email is
optional for bug reports. Free text may contain information you choose to send.
Your browser sends the form to Cloudflare for validation, then directly to
[Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/privacy) for delivery to the maintainer's
[Gmail inbox](https://policies.google.com/privacy). These services receive
the fields and relevant network information; Web3Forms and its infrastructure
and spam-filtering providers may process IP/email information under their own
policies. Processing may occur internationally.

The purposes are bug investigation, requested support, privacy requests and
abuse prevention. The project does not use report details for advertising,
mailing lists, selling personal information or training its own AI models. The
Worker has no report database and does not deliberately log report bodies or
email addresses; provider logs and email copies can still exist.

Report email retention depends on investigation, correspondence, security and
applicable legal needs. Inbox deletion is manual; no automatic inbox-deletion
schedule is configured. Providers follow their own retention/deletion processes.
The website notice records Web3Forms' current maximum of three years unless
deleted earlier or a shorter plan period applies. Cloudflare Workers Logs
currently retain records for three days on Free plans or seven on Paid plans;
other provider/security records may differ. Deleting an email does not guarantee
immediate deletion of all provider copies or backups.

The form is intended for people aged 18 or older; younger users should ask a
parent or guardian to submit. Do not send passwords, API keys, financial/health
details, private save/account information or other people's personal data.
GitHub issues are a separate public action; review drafts/logs before posting.

## Multiplayer data is separate

Static inspection of this exact published DLL found that its lobby name is
derived from the **Windows account username**, sent to the host and shared in
party rosters. A direct TCP host receives connecting peers' network addresses.
Local connection/session logs may contain player names, host addresses and
session identifiers. Use trusted hosts and redact logs before public sharing.
The maintainer cannot remotely erase another participant's local logs through
a website privacy request. These findings do not establish every game, Steam,
loader or other-mod data practice; no blanket no-data-collection claim is made.
